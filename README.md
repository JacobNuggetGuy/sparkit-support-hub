# Sparkit Support

Welcome to the official support repository for Sparkit.

## About Sparkit

Sparkit is a platform designed to help users learn, create, and explore through interactive experiences and innovative technology.

## Contact Support

If you need assistance, have questions, or would like to report an issue, please contact us:

**Email:** [officialsparkitapp@gmail.com](mailto:officialsparkitapp@gmail.com)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I report a bug?

Send an email to our support team with details about the issue, including screenshots if possible.

### How do I suggest a feature?

We welcome feedback and feature suggestions. Please email our support team [officialsparkitapp@gmail.com](mailto:officialsparkitapp@gmail.com) or suggest the app: Profile >> Settings >> Make a Suggestion

### How do I contact the development team?

For general inquiries, please use the support email listed above.
